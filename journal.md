I spent three hours working on Tidbit, my custom audio synthesizer and sound-control device built around the Teensy 4.1. During this session,
I developed the PCB layout and worked on organizing the different components into a functional control surface.

I added the main rotary encoder section for master volume, tempo, tuning, cutoff, resonance, envelope modulation, decay, accent, reverb, and drive. I also worked on the placement of the waveform-selection switches, auxiliary controls, phase control, and push buttons, making sure the different sections were arranged clearly across the board.
<img width="3332" height="2362" alt="SCH_Schematic1_1-P1_2026-11-01 (1)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/21379d12-43ae-42d0-9247-e7afd61ddebd" />
I worked on the component footprints, PCB routing, and overall layout, connecting the control sections to the Teensy footprint while keeping the design organized. I also reviewed the 3D PCB render to understand how the components would sit on the finished board and how the physical interface would look when assembled.
<img width="882" height="463" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-01 022832" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9a09448c-240d-411a-8c25-a11f57f3df1d" />
This session helped establish the overall hardware layout of Tidbit and brought the different sound-control elements together into a more complete PCB design. The next stage will involve reviewing the routing and electrical connections, checking the design rules, and preparing the board for fabrication and eventual hardware testing.
